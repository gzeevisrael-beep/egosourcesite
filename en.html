A map of inner work according to Baal HaSulam and Rabash

The will to receive does not need to be destroyed. Its direction of use needs to change

The substance of creation remains the will to receive. The creature does not become a giver in its substance. Equivalence of form is attained through intention: for whom and for what purpose reception is used. Nature cannot rewrite itself by its own power. A need for correction can be prepared through the primary sources, the environment, and a common request.

01

The desire is not corrected by erasing it, but by changing how it is used

The will to receive is the substance of creation. Spiritual work therefore is not built on suppressing desire or trying to become a creature with another nature. The work begins in intention, which determines what the act of receiving serves.

Working principle: do not suppress the desire, justify it, or run from it. Use the revealed state to build a direction above personal calculation.
01

Desire

This is the substance of the vessel. Through desire, the created being can feel pleasure, lack, and a relation to the Source.

02

Intention

It determines the spiritual form of an action: whether desire serves only personal benefit or is directed toward bestowal and connection.

03

Helplessness

Discovering that I cannot change my nature by myself is not failure. From it, a genuine need for the correcting force can be born.

02

The outer world returns the material of my soul to me

Kabbalah treats perceived reality as a picture revealed within a person's desire. Separation, flaws, and opposites that appear outside me can therefore become material for correcting my relation to the common system.

This does not cancel facts, responsibility, or action in the material world. Inner work does not deny reality. It corrects the attitude toward what has been revealed.
01

Mirror

What strongly disturbs me in another person is useful first as material for my own clarification, not as permission to correct the other person.

02

Friends

In group work, the friends become the place where I learn to relate to separate desires as parts of one soul.

03

Multiplicity

Unity does not require sameness. The more differences are included in one direction, the richer the common vessel for revealing unity becomes.

03

The middle line: everything comes from above, and the work begins in my attitude

Do not choose between "There is none else besides Him" and responsibility

1

Attribute the state to the Creator

A thought, desire, disturbance, ascent, or emptiness is accepted as the material now revealed for the work.

2

Do not claim ownership or use it as an excuse

The phrase "everything is from the Creator" does not release me from the work. The question is not only where the state came from, but what I direct it toward.

3

Become included in the friends

Receive the importance of the goal from the environment, see the friends' aspiration, and place common connection above personal rightness and present feeling.

4

Raise a common request

Ask not to replace the circumstances with pleasant ones, but for the power to build above them an intention to bestow, connection, and one common appeal.

04

Three minutes of inner orientation

Turn a state into a common request

The practice does not replace a lesson, workshop, or work in the ten. It only helps direct the current state toward connection and appeal.

Clarification 1 of 3

Name the truth

Quietly acknowledge that the will to receive is ruling me now and distancing me from connection, and that I cannot change it directly.

The whole world is my soul

We do not collect identical people. We connect differences in one intention.

The ten gives a practical model of one soul. Each person retains a distinct desire, character, and perception. Correction does not come from erasing opposites, but from building connection above them. Multiplicity then becomes a condition for a fuller revelation of unity.
